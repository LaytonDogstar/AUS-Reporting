Passed filters is leads sent to the lender over leads it was offered: how many get through its filters. Accepted is how many of those it bought. Redirected is how many sales made it to the lender’s own page. Each line is a rolling seven days; the figure beside it is the latest week, and the change is against the weeks before it. ▼ in red marks a fall of at least 10% that is beyond what chance would produce, judged only where the latest week had 50 or more. Profit is the lender report’s: revenue less affiliate commission. It does not take off bank statement costs, which are charged per lead on the affiliate side, or the internal sources’ costs, so it reads higher than the affiliate figures. A band whose share bought sits well above its share offered is a customer this lender favours; one well below is a customer it avoids.
