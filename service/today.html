Why earnings per sale are where they are

day · typical		 MixPriceEffect on EPL

EPL is revenue ÷ sales. Mix is a lender taking a bigger or smaller share of the sales than usual, valued at its usual price. Price is a lender paying more or less per sale than usual. Mix plus price is each lender's effect, and the effects add up to the whole gap.
